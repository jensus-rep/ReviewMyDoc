# ReviewMyDoc-Logo

Generiert mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug (kein CLI-Aufruf). Das transparente PNG
liegt unter `src/ReviewMyDoc.Web/wwwroot/images/reviewmydoc-logo.png` und wird in beiden
Seitenrahmen sowie als Favicon verwendet. Der Markenname bleibt zugänglicher HTML-Text.

## Verwendeter Prompt

Create one polished minimalist app logo icon for ReviewMyDoc, a calm premium document editing and collaborative review application. A distinctive single navy blue rounded document silhouette with a gently folded upper right corner and an elegant small integrated checkmark in the lower half, a subtle second offset sheet suggesting collaboration. Geometric, clean, confident, exceptionally legible at 32 pixels, restrained soft dimensional highlights, mostly flat graphic design. Dark navy #123a5f with one muted steel blue accent. No text, no letters, no wordmark, no mockup, no framing tile, no cast shadow beyond icon. True transparent alpha background, not a checkerboard drawing. Single centered mark fills approximately 85 percent of square canvas with even padding. Finished production logo asset.
